# Originality and name-swap

After the new system is on a cover (if any) and one representative operate page, swap the product nouns for another domain. If the screen still fits, the makeover is a theme, not a product. Push copy, moments, and material tint until the swap fails.

Also fail if every makeover you produce would share one layout and one grey+accent recipe regardless of audience.

Do not plagiarize a reference site's layout even when you correctly took its green.
