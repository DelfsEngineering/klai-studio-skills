# Scenario: phone-first field app
Setup: an app used on phones away from a desk (in a vehicle or on site), with a list of jobs, a detail page, and a completion flow.

Expected:
- Profile names the device and setting and says what the user needs at a glance.
- Quality pass starts at phone width; touch targets and type suit the profile; the main list shows without scrolling past decoration.
- The full completion flow is clicked end to end on mock data.
- Times show in local time, not UTC; celebration effects only on a real completion.
