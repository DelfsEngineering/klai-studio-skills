# Originality and name-swap

A brief that still fits after you rename the product is a template. Run this before calling direction done.

Adapted in own words from Unslop's template-look checks (CC BY 4.0; credit in [LICENSE-THIRD-PARTY.md](../LICENSE-THIRD-PARTY.md)). Changes: Klai operate/cover split; no requirement to generate marketing scenes; guesses stay labelled.

## Name-swap
1. Take the cover headline (if any), the character line, the signature moments, and one operate page title + empty state.
2. Replace the product and domain nouns with a different domain (a bakery if this is dispatch; a clinic if this is a florist cooler).
3. If the sentences still work, rewrite with this product's nouns, tools, and setting until they fail the swap.

"Organize your day with clarity" fails. "Assign the next unassigned delivery before the van leaves the dock" passes.

## Other template tells
- Neutral grey UI + a leftover platform indigo button, on a product whose world is warm, physical, or nocturnal.
- The same hero structure and palette you used on the previous unrelated app (habit, not evidence).
- A signature moment that is "a gradient blob" or "a generic dashboard screenshot".
- Character words from the banned list.

## Originality vs plagiarism
Matching authorized brand tokens is required when the owner asked for it. Matching a reference site's layout is not. If you notice you have reproduced a distinctive composition, discard that composition and keep only tokens.

## Record
Write the swap you tried, whether it failed (good), and what you changed. Two sentences is enough.
