# Worked examples (illustrative, do not copy)

Three generic products. Invented. Not clients. Do not reuse their palettes or headlines on a real app.

## 1. Desk dispatch board (operate)

**Evidence.** Stated: afternoon dispatchers, desk monitors, phone in the other hand. Observed: a table of jobs with status, late flag, driver. Guess: fluorescent office light.

**Direction (abridged).**
- Jobs: assign the next job without opening a detail unless the late flag is on.
- Character: `exact, dry, a little warm` — warm paper-neutral, not orange panels.
- World: clipboards, bay doors, ballpoint; tint neutrals slightly warm; accent only on Late and the assign control.
- Moments: (1) Late column (2) driver availability strip. Quiet: filters, nav.
- Copy before layout: empty = "No unassigned jobs for this shift"; primary = "Assign".
- Name-swap with a spa booking grid fails because of Late/bay/shift nouns.

**Not polish:** the table is readable but the brand is wrong → makeover. **Not remix:** they did not ask to add routing or a new job.

## 2. Cover for a scheduling product (marketing) + calendar (operate)

**Evidence.** Public site authorized. Observed: deep green header CTA `#1F4B3A`, serif wordmark on marketing only. Inner app is a week grid.

**Direction.**
- Cover character: `plain, horticultural, daylight`. Calendar character: `exact, dense, same green as selection only`.
- Moments: cover uses one real shop-floor photo the owner supplied; calendar's moment is today-column emphasis. No photo inside the grid.
- Copy: cover headline names who books what ("Cut-flower studios book cooler time by the hour"), not "Scheduling reimagined".
- Tokens: green from the public CTA (provenance URL); serif on cover only; operate UI one sans.

**Handoff:** makeover may restyle cover and calendar tokens; remix would be wrong if flows stay.

## 3. Phone field checklist (operate, outdoor)

**Evidence.** Observed: large complete button, three statuses. Stated: used in sun, gloves off but dirty fingers. Guess: 40+ age.

**Direction.**
- Character: `high-contrast, physical, impatient`.
- World: gravel, sky glare — dark text on light sand surfaces, not glass dark mode unless they work at night (they do not).
- Moments: the Complete control and the pass/fail stamp. Quiet: the list chrome.
- Layout consequence: 48px primary, no ghost buttons, no tiny trailing icons as the only tap target.
- Name-swap with a meditation app fails (Complete/pass-fail/glare).

If they also want a new "photo of the install" step, that is a new job → remix, not direction-plus-makeover.
